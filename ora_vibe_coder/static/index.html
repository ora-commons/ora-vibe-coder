Welcome to Ora Vibe Coder

Vibe finds your projects automatically inside your Documents folder. This page belongs to the supplied installer; ordinary use starts from the Ora Vibe Coder launcher.

Python 3.10 or later runs this local interface. Your coding tool handles conversations, account login, and approvals.

Ora AI Boost (Gear 3 and Gear 4) is an optional extra for Specification and Planning — Vibe works without it. Its releases are at Ora AI Boost releases.

Working with Vibe Coder

Vibe searches this installation's configured projects folder, or your Documents folder when none is configured, for projects with a Project.md definition; Refresh projects, in Open existing, repeats that search during a session. Open an ordinary folder; its saved documents appear in the reader. Code, Checks, and Baseline stay explicit selections.

Steps 1–3 choose a project, show its details, and choose a coding tool. Step 4 works on the active stage, regardless of which file is selected in the reader: Ask AI to work on the Specification, Plan, or implementation submits your text, up to ten pictures, and up to ten selected Markdown or text files through the selected coding tool. Selected files are included for this message only and stay in place; your message is saved before it is shown, and the complete answer appears when the turn finishes — no streaming. Enter sends; Shift+Enter adds a line; your input-method composition is respected. AI questions arrive in the same conversation and are answered there. One work turn runs per project at a time; reading files and switching projects stay available while it works. A completed turn has a plain status line; Bridge diagnostics needing no action sit under collapsed Technical details, while failures and notices needing your attention stay visible. Copy stage request for another AI prepares the same current-stage instructions without replacing the saved request file.

The document pane shows your saved files and refreshes them automatically when they change on disk — including saves made by your coding tool. It keeps a file you explicitly selected; otherwise Build & verify shows the Verification Report when available, then the Programming Result. On a wide window the panes stay side by side; drag their divider to adjust their widths. On a narrow window, Show document and Show conversation switch between them. In Specification and Plan, step 5 runs one fresh assessment only when you choose Review, and saves its verdict; drag the divider below the conversation to give replies more room. With Second opinion selected, the authoring model assesses, a second harness of your choice evaluates, and the author revises — optionally iterating to agreement (at most three rounds). The interface states lab diversity honestly — different labs, same lab, or unconfirmed when the passes did not report their actual model/provider identity — and reports any disagreement that remains; you break the ties. Sending a chat message alone does not record a review. The advisory assessment — COMPLETE, INCOMPLETE with noted deficiencies, or NOT REVIEWED — is shown by the document. Step 6 prepares the next action as a saved, inspectable request that you can open in an interactive coding-tool session or copy. Before implementation advises you when the Specification or Plan lacks a COMPLETE review for its current version; Continue with implementation still proceeds. The optional external review action prepares an inspectable request; preparing it does not run the review.

Build & verify has three actions after the implementation conversation: step 5 asks its AI to save a truthful Programming Result, step 6 asks a coordinator to arrange fresh independent verification and save one Verification Report, and step 7 corrects the implementation and updates the Programming Result, then stops; choose step 6 again for fresh independent verification. Each action lets you send one turn without an automatic time limit in Vibe Coder or copy the full instructions into an existing AI chat. A Vibe turn shows status and its final reply here; an external chat remains visible in its coding tool. The implementation agent cannot serve as its own independent verifier. The saved report supplies the displayed verdict; with none saved, Build & verify shows NOT VERIFIED. A saved verdict reflects the work actually reviewed, and the report's presence alone does not mean the implementation passed.

Readiness of the selected tool is checked in the background without a model call; failures name their own reason, and sign-in or native permissions are resolved in the tool's own window — never labeled as login failures automatically.

Closing the browser leaves Vibe and external AI work running. Stop Vibe warns when a turn is active or you have unsent text or selected files. If you continue, it interrupts the active turn, records that outcome, and ends its owned processes; files already changed remain. Mac app Quit follows the same warning and interruption path. Reopen from the app icon to return to the workspace; an interrupted turn is never replayed automatically.
