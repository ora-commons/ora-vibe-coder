Welcome to Ora Vibe Coder

Vibe finds your projects automatically inside your Documents folder. This page belongs to the supplied installer; ordinary use starts from the Ora Vibe Coder launcher.

Python 3.10 or later runs this local interface. Your coding tool handles conversations, account login, and approvals.

Ora AI Boost (Gear 3 and Gear 4) is bundled with Vibe as an optional aid for Specification and Planning — Vibe works without it, and setup installs its reviewed runtime beside the application.

Working with Ora Vibe Coder

Choose a project, inspect its Details, and choose your coding tool. Specification, Plan, and Build & verify stay available throughout the work. Your conversation and saved documents sit beside one another; on narrow windows, Show document and Show conversation switch the visible pane.

The status row names the work actually dispatched: Run to Completion when applicable, stage, activity, and the current action's minutes:seconds. Green means active work. Yellow Waiting explains the condition and next action; its timer freezes. Stopped and Finished are neutral endpoints, and do not mean a document passed. Document headings keep their independent review verdicts.

Write a message, add pictures or text files, and send it. The complete answer appears when the turn finishes. Answer to continue shows questions from the response. Review has its own Reviewer choice; Second opinion adds an independent evaluation. Approve records approval without starting work. Request repairs sends the findings the AI can fix, while decisions remain yours.

The next stage offers Send, Open in Terminal, or Copy request. Saved request controls beside the stage retain the exact file path, manual copy, and desktop route. External review file stays beside Review. A prepared or opened request does not prove receipt or completion.

Run to Completion follows Specification, Plan, implementation, independent verification, and corrections. It pauses for your answers or a condition needing attention. A temporary failure is retried only when safe; a dispatched step may already have changed files, so Inspect saved work brings you to the retained response. Read the files, stop the paused run, then request only the remaining work in the conversation. Continue is available when a step has not dispatched. Nothing substitutes a different tool or bypasses sign-in and permission checks.

Stop Action stops only the selected project's action or its entire Completion Run, including future steps. Other projects keep running. Files already changed remain; an unfinished answer may be lost. Stop is not Undo and never replays. Quit Vibe in Settings, or the Mac's normal Quit, warns about active work and unsent text before closing Vibe. Closing the browser leaves Vibe running.

Tool readiness and discovery run automatically. Actionable sign-in, permission, or component problems explain what to resolve; technical records remain available to the AI. Settings also holds role, model, effort, and interruption choices.
