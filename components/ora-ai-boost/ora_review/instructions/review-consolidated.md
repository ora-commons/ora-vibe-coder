# Review of the consolidated answer

Review the complete consolidated answer as the exact deliverable the user will
receive. Apply the common material-defect and overlooked-consideration
questions, and also ask whether consolidation:

- lost distinct supported material from either lane;
- injected an unsupported claim or treated agreement as proof;
- created false compromise or hid a real uncertainty, condition, or decision;
- retained repetition or irrelevant alternatives; or
- damaged the user's commitment, voice, requested form, or practical utility.

The answer may select one conclusion and may also preserve unresolved tension
when that is the most truthful result. Recommend changes with reasons; do not
command a mechanical merger of the lanes.
