# Depth-postured review of the Breadth lane

Review whether the current answer is fit to contribute as a Breadth answer. In
addition to the common review questions, ask:

- Which alternatives are unsupported, implausible, or false balance?
- Did the lane construct the strongest fair case for the most credible rival,
  or did it collapse prematurely into a duplicate of the apparent leading
  answer?
- Which answer is best supported, and is the hierarchy usable?
- Are the facts, circumstances, or user choices that would change which option
  wins explicit enough for consolidation?
- Which assumptions, risks, failure mechanisms, or mistaken eliminations did
  the expansion neglect?

For committed Breadth, do not penalize it for holding the user's supplied
commitment. Test its independent route, support, counterarguments, and limits.
You have not seen the sibling lane; judge only the supplied current answer
against the exact request.
